## 2026-10-14 — Phase 8 — TlcMonthUnpublished: a TLC month unpublished past its usual lag alerts (#89)

- Done: new metadata DB collector `airflow-publication-waits.collector.yml` exports `airflow_publication_wait_interval_end_seconds{dag_id, run_id, data_month, file}` for every `wait_published` instance that is deferred, retrying or failed, valued at its run's data interval end. New rule `TlcMonthUnpublished` (`airflow-rules.yaml`, group `data.products`) fires when such a month closed over 100 days ago (`for: 1h`), with promtool tests (`tests/monitoring/tlc-publication.test.yaml`). Grafana "Airflow overview" gains "TLC months awaiting publication" (days since month close, yellow at 84, red at 100). Runbook: Phase 8 entry 3. PLAN.md D35 records the threshold and why; LESSONS.md one line.
- Evidence: the collector's SQL against the lab's metadata DB (Airflow 3.3.1 schema) returns the 2026-09 run's yellow and green waits, deferred, at 2026-10-01; the real `sql_exporter` 0.24.9 with this branch's config exports both series. `make rules-test`: quiet at 99 days, pending at 100d1h, firing at 100d2h, resolving after the wait succeeds, other DAGs ignored; the test fails when the threshold moves to 98 days. Runbook diagnosis commands run on the lab: the source answers 403 for 2026-09 and 200 for 2026-08; EL Factory answers `not_published`. `make lint`, `make test`.
- Decisions: 100 days from month close, from the measured 24-83-day lag in `docs/research/tlc-source-facts.md`, about 20 days before the sensor's 120-day timeout. Only waits already told "not published" count, so a queued backfill of an old month never fires. The threshold is a constant in the rule, not a warehouse row as D37's deadlines: it describes a source, not a product contract. Recovery for a month TLC drops for good: mark the waits `skipped` through the REST API (the UI's "Mark as" offers only success and failed).

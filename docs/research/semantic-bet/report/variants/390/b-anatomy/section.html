What the question sets hold

Every question the setups were asked, one mark each, set by set. A mark's colour is its group, a hollow struck mark is a question retired from scoring, and a bar under a mark means its obvious SQL gives another number. The two versions of the hard set are drawn as one: a thread joins each of v1's questions to its copy in v2.

Open a mark to read its question, the numbers its answer must carry, and every setup's runs on it.

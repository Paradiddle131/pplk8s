# Variant A: a tab strip (#396)

- **Grouping, seven tabs in today's order**: Summary (abstract, who wins) · Decision map (Fig. 1) · MetricFlow agent (Figs. 2–5) · Every run (Fig. 6) · Setups (map and explorer together) · Question sets (the appendix of more sets) · Method & glossary.
- **The abstract moves into Summary**, so the strip sits right under the title and byline: the reader sees at a glance that the report has parts, and every tab opens at its own top instead of below an abstract repeated on every tab.
- **The strip**: small capitals on one ink rule, with the open tab marked by a 3 px stretch of that rule (a running head, not a set of buttons). It sticks to the top, and the map's and the plate's sticky heads stop under it. Its figure tags ("Figs. 2–5") show the figures' numbering on desktop.
- **On a phone** the strip scrolls sideways inside itself, keeps the open tab in view, and fades at the edge where more tabs hide.
- **How a reader moves**: by click, by arrow keys, Home and End (WAI-ARIA tabs, roving tabindex, activation follows focus), or by "Next: … →" at the foot of each part.
- **Links and history**: each tab has a hash (`#summary`, `#decision-map`, `#metricflow`, `#every-run`, `#setups`, `#question-sets`, `#method`). `#term-*`, `#map-*`, `#setup-*`, `#setup-map` and `#other-set-versions` open their tab. Every switch is a history entry that remembers its scroll position, so Back, Forward and reload return to the same place. A run of arrow-key moves leaves one entry.
- **The tooltip's jumps** ("More in the glossary ↓", "See its row in the map ↓") open the target's tab, scroll to the target and flash it. "↑ Back" (or the browser's Back) returns to the tab and the place, with focus on the term.
- **Figures**: closed tabs are folded to zero height (hidden, inert, opacity 0) but stay laid out at the column's width. So every figure draws at its real width when first shown and redraws on a resize while hidden, with no change to the drawing code; sizes match the one-page report at 390, 900 and 1180 px.
- **Trade-offs**: find-in-page searches only the open tab. Seven tabs is one more than the six asked for. A 45 px strip costs the setup map's sticky readout its room at 1280×800: the longest text ends 34 px below the fold, where the one-page report had 10 px to spare. It still fits at 1280×900 and 1440×900 (13 to 14 px to spare). The fix is one line in the setup map's `fit()`: subtract `--tabh` from `innerHeight`.
- **#390's question section** goes in Question sets, ahead of the appendix, which it can absorb. A plain row of questions also sits naturally one tab after Every run, where the plate shows every run per question.
- **Built from** this directory's `template.html` only; the sections are untouched. `build.py` inserts the appendix with the setup sections, so the template's script moves it into Question sets; a second placeholder in `build.py` would do this at build time.
- **Asks**: the appendix's text says "Sets the figures above do not draw"; in a tab this should read "Sets the other parts do not draw" (`build.py`). No new glossary terms are needed.
- **Checks**: Playwright in Chromium, 90 checks (`checks/test.js`, `checks/test2.js`), 0 failures, 0 console errors.
- **Clipped text**: the stock `tools/clipcheck.js` times out clicking a set switch inside a closed tab. `clipcheck-views.js`, a copy that opens each tab before checking it and its controls, finds 0 problems at 1280, 1024, 768, 400 and 360 px in light, dark and the dark button, with no horizontal scroll. It checks every state from the page's top: the overlap test is geometry only and would flag text passing under the stuck strip, while the paint-aware covered test passes.
- **Not verified**: Safari and Firefox; real touch devices; screen-reader output.

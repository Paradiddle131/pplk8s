# Variant C: an overview with chapters

- **Overview** (`#overview`, or no hash): title, byline, abstract, winners band, then "The report, chapter by chapter": one entry per chapter with its number, name, a dotted leader to its figure links, and the takeaway of its lead figure read from the page (`#t1`, `#t2`, `#t4`, `#t6` for the plate's current set; counts from the data for the rest).
- **Chapters**, in today's order: 1 The decision map (`#decision-map`, Fig. 1) · 2 Where the ADK agent on MetricFlow stands (`#where-metricflow-stands`, Figs. 2–3) · 3 Why: covered against uncovered (`#covered-uncovered`, Figs. 4–5) · 4 Every run (`#every-run`, Fig. 6) · 5 The setups (`#setups`: the map, then the explorer) · 6 Appendix: more question sets (`#more-question-sets`) · 7 Method and glossary (`#method`), last.
- **Moving**: an entry (or one of its figure links) opens the chapter as its own view; each chapter has a quiet head ("← Overview · Chapter 2 of 7", theme switch) and a foot with the previous and next chapters (the overview at either end). Every move is a history entry: Back and Forward restore the view and scroll position; "← Overview" lands on the chapter's entry with focus on it; a chapter opens with focus on its title. `#fig3`, `#map-<arm>`, `#term-<key>`, `#setup-<arm>` and `#other-set-versions` open their chapter and scroll to the target (rows and terms flash).
- **Jumps**: "More in the glossary ↓" and "See its row in the map ↓" open the target's chapter as a history entry and flash the target; "↑ Back" (or the browser's Back) returns to the view, the scroll position and the focused term.
- **Figures**: a view not shown stays laid out at the column's width (`height: 0; visibility: hidden; inert`), so every figure measures its real width while away and after a resize; on showing, a figure whose width changed is redrawn and the setup sections get a `resize` event. Chapter elements carry `view-<name>` ids, so the browser's own fragment scroll never fights the router.
- **Trade-offs**: the overview stays one scroll (about 2,200 px at 1280 × 900): the contents start below the winners, not on the first screen. Links, not tabs: no arrow-key pattern; Tab and Enter, Back and Forward do the work. All views load and lay out at once, as the long page does today. One reading order, chapters dipped into from the overview; no running list of chapters inside a chapter.
- **#390's question section**: chapter 6 becomes "The question sets": #390's section first (what each set tests, its groups, every question one click away), then today's appendix tables; its entry's takeaway would come from that section.
- **Checked** in Chromium (Playwright): no console errors; every view by click, keyboard and URL; reload; Back and Forward with scroll restored; both jumps and "↑ Back" across views; a resize to 900 px and to 390 px while away draws Figures 2–6 identical to a fresh load and the explorer narrow; set switches, a glider, the theme switch in a chapter; no horizontal scroll at 390 px; new text 4.86:1 or more in both themes (hover included); no motion under reduced motion.
- **Clipped text**: the stock `tools/clipcheck.js --quick` checks the overview, then times out clicking a set switch inside a closed chapter. `clipviews.js` here is a copy that loads each view by its URL and drives only visible controls; over all eight views, five widths and three themes (120 loads, every visible set switch, explorer setup, map target and glider) it finds 0 problems, no console errors and no horizontal scroll.

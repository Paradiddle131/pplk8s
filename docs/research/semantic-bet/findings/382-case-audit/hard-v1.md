## Defects and doubts, most severe first

1. **hard_happiest_call_topic: defect (fixture).** The answer is fixed by construction: product is the only topic with any positive call, and 3 of 4 product calls are positive in every week, so every period, all data, and the mean of daily shares all give 0.75. Retire.
2. **hard_cost_per_booking_rise: defect (scorer).** The expected 27.00 is the last day of the week asked about; a wrong-channel answer that writes the week as "Sep 21–27" or "21/09–27/09" is scored right. Retire.
3. **hard_orders_last_fiscal_quarter: defect (scorer).** The expected 217 is the order table's whole row count: the fiscal quarter spans all the fixture's order days, so an unfiltered count passes, and so does a calendar-Q3 answer that also states the table size. Retire; a fiscal-period case needs data that a fiscal boundary cuts.
4. **hard_customer_base_turkey: defect (scorer).** The expected 5 is tiny, three other countries also have 5, and the "end of September" date never bites (no TR signups after 12 Sep). Retire.
5. **hard_active_customers_week: doubt (scorer).** Both meanings are small numbers (10, 9), and the 28-day engaged count (10) collides with the order reading, so a weekly-plus-monthly engagement answer passes without the order meaning.
6. **hard_refund_rate_by_value: doubt (readings).** "Orders placed" with or without cancelled ones gives 1.34% or 1.15%; the case accepts only the first, except in fraction form, where the 0.006 absolute tolerance accepts both.
7. **hard_replies_within_12_hours: doubt (scorer).** 50% is a common number, and the end-exclusive slip gives 50% too.
8. **hard_best_customer_september: label doubt (v1 and v2).** Possibly covered via the `customer` entity group-by; settled by the arms' query tools. Also reads the planted customer 10 as runner-up.
9. **hard_repeat_rate_october: v2 label doubt.** `repeat_customers` ÷ `customers_distinct` gives the same 10/17, so composable on the fixture, though the metric's lifetime definition is not the question's.
10. **hard_ad_spend_wow, hard_candidates_still_in_running, hard_roas_september: doubt (scorer).** A right answer in a natural form fails the parser: "6%" (6.007%), "33%" (one third), "2,056%" (ROAS as a percentage).
11. **hard_fiscal_ytd_net_revenue: doubt (fixture).** The number is right, but the fiscal start never bites (calendar YTD gives the same), so the case does not test what makes it fiscal.
12. **hard_mqls_social_september (40), hard_top_channel_bookings (31), hard_runner_up_channel_leads (20): doubt (scorer).** Small round counts of the kind BRIEF § 4 names; I found no concrete wrong reading that gives them, so I could not settle these. The owner's policy on small integer answers would.

Set-wide scorer notes (not per-case defects, but they touch the cases above):

- `ASKS_WHICH` matches any sentence with "which", "or" or "mean" that ends in "?", so an ambiguous turn answered with a single silent pick plus a closing offer ("Want it by week or by month?") is scored right. This affects all five ambiguous turns: hard_ambiguous_q3, hard_real_revenue, hard_active_customers_week, hard_sales_germany_this_month and hard_last_seven_days.
- A rate is matched within 0.006 absolute in fraction form, a wide band on small rates (±45% on hard_refund_rate_by_value's 0.0134, ±10% on hard_followup_cancelling_country's 0.0606).
- A rate rounded to a whole percentage matches only when the rate is within 0.006 points of that integer.

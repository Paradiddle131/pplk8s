Written 2026-11-06T13:47Z.
Every number below comes from `docker exec pplk8s-warehouse-1 psql -U warehouse -d dbt_dev -At -c "<sql>"`.

Limit: each `blind` entry names a draft id (V1, W12, D8, ...), but the draft text lives in the finding under `findings/`, which the brief forbids.

## Defects and doubts, most severe first

1. **hv2_first_order_value_october (defect, readings).** "First week of October" gives 36.5, 42.5 or 32.0 on a Monday-week set; only 36.5 is accepted. Retire it.
2. **hv2_signups_never_ordered (defect, scorer).** The expected 16 is also a customer id (`Customer 16`) in the list a wrong answer (13) would print, and a small count. Retire it. Its rubric claim also fails: the miss comes from the as-of anchor, not the anti-join.
3. **hv2_net_revenue_week_returns_credited (defect, label).** It is not a trap: the obvious query is the expected SQL. The naive contradicts the question. Relabel it to `covered`, dropping `trap` and `sql_hard`. Its scoring is sound.
4. **hv2_top_three_customers_share (defect, label), and hv2_median_customer_spend's label.** Both are one grouped metric query plus arithmetic, which the set calls `composable` for hv2_country_lowest_daily_revenue. Relabel both to `composable`, or relabel the country case to `uncovered`, but use one rule. This moves cases between the groups that criteria 2 and 3 read. The top-three case's "window" rubric is also not exercised (no ties).
5. **hv2_median_customer_spend (doubt, readings).** It has an even number of customers, so the answer depends on the median convention. The house's own median (`APPROX_QUANTILES`, BigQuery) likely returns a middle value, not 1891.6. Settle it with one BigQuery query.
6. **hv2_median_gap_between_orders (doubt, fixture).** 80% of the gaps are the fixture's constant 27 h, so the median is fixed by construction for any partitioned method. Settle whether that counts as "fixed by construction".
7. **hv2_spend_on_days_without_email_booking (doubt, label).** Whether filtering one per-day metric result by another counts as composable. Its sql_hard claim is not shown: the recorded naive is a strawman, and the obvious `not exists` is right.
8. **Provenance, not a scoring defect.** The rewrites of V11 (orders to calls) and V34 (a different question altogether), and in part V9 and V12, were chosen with knowledge of the fixture. They belong under `post`, as v1's post-hoc cases do, not under `blind`.
9. **The scorer's tolerance on fractions (set-wide, minor).** 0.006 absolute on a rate stated as a fraction accepts nearby wrong readings: unreturned share including cancelled orders (0.9566 vs 0.9521), top three net of returns (0.3492 vs 0.3513). Answers given in percent are not affected.

Counts: ok 8, defect 4, doubt 3 (15 cases).

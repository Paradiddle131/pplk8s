# The eval sets and their runs, audited for defects (#382)

Question: which cases of the three sets test something other than what they claim, and which logged runs mislead, so that the bet's numbers rest only on sound tests?
The owner, 2026-11-06, in a session: "if you notice any errors on the questions, I'd rather them gone"; "run the blind defect audit before #380 ... across the entire results ... redact them, remove them, and rerun them"; on the retirements, "Retire all, make sure the final report doesn't care about what happened earlier and uses the latest the freshest data points only"; on the scorer, "Fix scorer + redact".

## What was run

- **Blind case audit:** four subagents, one per range (frozen 1–48, frozen 49–96, v1, hard-v2's 15 new cases).
  None of them read a log, result, report or finding with numbers.
  Each reran every expected, naive and legacy SQL on dbt_dev, re-derived each answer from `silver.*`, and tested the scorer's patterns on sample answers.
  The brief and the four reports: [`382-case-audit/`](382-case-audit/) (`BRIEF.md`, `frozen-a.md`, `frozen-b.md`, `hard-v1.md`, `hard-v2-new.md`).
- **Results audit:** one subagent, after the case audit, over every run of every arm, model and set.
  It ran mechanical flags, then read 1,638 flagged right turns and 151 flagged not-right turns by hand.
  It covers frozen 7,553 runs, v1 3,222 and hard-v2's 181 new-case runs; carried rows count once.
  The brief, the report and the candidates: [`382-case-audit/RESULTS-BRIEF.md`](382-case-audit/RESULTS-BRIEF.md), [`results.md`](382-case-audit/results.md), [`redact-candidates.jsonl`](382-case-audit/redact-candidates.jsonl).

## What was found (`measured`)

- **Case defects:** frozen 38 and 5 doubts of 96; v1 4 and 10 doubts of 43; hard-v2's new cases 4 (two of them labels) and 3 doubts.
- **Most scorer defects share one cause.** The eval's scorer counts any number in the answer, so small numbers (2, 5, 9, 10, a day, a customer ID) pass by chance.
  It also misses dates written as "Oct 2" or "Sept 30", right answers in words or whole percentages, multi-word declines, and definitions put in the answer's own words.
- **Runs that misled:** 208.
  - 121 chance carries; 63 of them are one case, `bet_wetter_day_nl_orders`, with a tie built into the fixture.
  - 86 right answers the parser missed.
  - 1 empty answer, scored wrong.
- **What did not happen:** the loose ambiguous-turn check never let a silent pick through (531 turns).
- **A fixture bug:** dbt_dev's candidate snapshot holds only each candidate's last stage. Filed as #383.

## What was done (the owner's go, 2026-11-06)

- **23 cases retired**, each with its reason in RULE.md § Retiring a defective case and in the overlay [`redacted.jsonl`](../redacted.jsonl): 20 frozen, 2 v1 (also in hard-v2), 1 hard-v2.
  The frozen set now scores 76 cases, 15 of them `uncovered`; v1 scores 41 and hard-v2 55.
- **122 runs redacted:** the 121 chance carries and the empty answer. 91 of them belong to retired cases.
  Of the 31 left, the data agents' 4 were rerun the same day (four gated passes, $0.000010).
  The free model's 3 are rerun by the lane.
  The 21 Claude API runs and 3 Claude Code subagent runs are not rerun (the API lane is closed), so those arms keep fewer runs on those questions.
- **4 labels corrected** in the overlay: two frozen cases are not distractors, one hard-v2 case is not a trap, one is `composable`.
- **The bet's scorer fixed** (RULE.md § Changes since freezing, 2026-11-06), for every arm.
  - Every log is scored again on read in full, from the expected numbers cached once from the fixture (`expected.json`).
  - All 63 audited misses in kept cases now read right.
  - Small expected numbers stand by the code only as the answer's first figure in an answer that does not decline; otherwise the judge decides.
  - Paraphrased definitions go to the judge.
- **About 265 turns now wait for the judge** (about 530 free requests). The lane's first step clears them; until then they count as wrong.
- **The report reads only `scored-runs.jsonl` and `results.json`**, as `bet_eval` scores them now, never the logs.

## What it means for the verdict

- No set's top arm changed. On the frozen set the free model's `native-mcp`, `custom-metricflow` and `custom-hybrid` read 89.5% each against the managed best's 92.1%, before the judge's backlog; the pairs are recomputed with every rebuild.
- The frozen set's uncovered group rested on a thin fixture: 14 of its 29 cases were defective. Its 15 left are reported with that n.
  A later version on a richer fixture would rebuild it.
- hard-v2's criteria (#380) are read on 14 new cases now, 8 of them `uncovered`.

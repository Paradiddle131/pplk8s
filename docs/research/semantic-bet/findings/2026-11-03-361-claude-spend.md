# Claude spend ledger

The running account of the owner's $100.00 of Anthropic credits for the bet ([#361](../../../../../../issues/361), plan in [the runner's finding](2026-11-03-361-claude-in-the-runner.md)).
Every Claude call's `usage` is priced at the published prices (`services/semantic-agent/src/semantic_agent/spend.py`, from Anthropic's pricing page read 2026-11-03) and logged, one line a call, to [`2026-11-03-361-claude-calls.jsonl`](2026-11-03-361-claude-calls.jsonl); the runner appends one row per pass below (`spend.finish_pass`), its running total summed from that calls log.
The Anomalies column holds the owner's checks run after every pass (#361, 2026-11-03): a pass over its budget; a call or case-run whose tokens are over three times the cost probe's per-case median on its model; any `credit balance` or billing error; any 429 (the plan expects none); a key refusal; the running total past $80.
The ledger cannot see the credit balance (that takes an Admin API key, kept out of the environment): compare its running total with the Console's remaining credits (Billing); a balance lower than $100 minus the running total means spend outside the runner.

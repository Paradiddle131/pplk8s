## What it means for the verdict

- On what is logged, the rule's step 2 reads "not worth building": against the managed best, `native-context`, ours shows no accuracy difference and no confident-wrong difference.
- It is far from final: the 47 cases are almost all `covered`, where a semantic layer should do best, and hold only 2 of the 29 `uncovered` cases, where `custom-metricflow` declines and loses accuracy (RULE.md § Scoring a run), so the full set should move ours down against `native-context`, not up.
- Against the managed arms below `native-context`, ours is ahead by about 0.3 in accuracy and below them in confident-wrong on these cases: what decides is how good the best managed configuration is, not whether a data agent can do this at all.
- The model gap is not separated yet: until ours runs on Claude (the check), a tie between ours on the free model and Google's agent on Google's model cannot be read as the semantic layer adding nothing.
